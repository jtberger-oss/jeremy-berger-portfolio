Men’s work and 1x1 coaching

Weekly groups and individual coaching for reclaiming the fullness of being human.

PracticeMen’s Group + 1x1 Coaching

FormatWeekly · In-Person and Online

DisciplineEmotional Intelligence, Fierce Presence, Honest Relationships

I offer groups and 1x1 coaching for groups and individuals looking to reclaim their lives and vitality. My work is rooted in what I’ve discovered about the human experience with my mentor Bruce Sanguin, whose mentor is Andrew Feldmár, who studied with psychiatrist R.D. Laing in London in the mid-20th century. See the interview series my partner Tara and I recorded with him in Vancouver here.

I help people come back to their direct experience of life—emotions, sensations, enjoyment, particular genius or soul gift. This usually involves grief for ways they’ve lived that have been small and false, what was necessary to adapt to childhood and the limitations of the world we live in. It’s a beautiful journey and my great pleasure to be with clients in this way. The impact of the work ripples out through the full experience of life: relationship with self, others, work, and spirituality.

My men’s work partner and I have hosted a weekly group at my house every Monday night for nearly two years. We offer a place to practice being fully human in the loving and at times confronting presence of others. This group has now ended and we are enrolling for a year-long group and course beginning in Q1 of 2027.

R.D. Laing wrote, “We all live on the hope that authentic meeting between human beings can still occur.” I aim to create spaces for this and remove the obstacles that stand in the way.
